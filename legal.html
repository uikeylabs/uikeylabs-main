Legal Policies, Terms of Service, Privacy & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 • Applicable to all web engineering services and digital utilities provided by UIKEY LABS (Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh, India).

1. Scope of Service & Delivery Timeline

UIKEY LABS designs and builds custom responsive websites, industry landing pages, and WhatsApp inquiry funnels. Standard launch timelines (typically 3 to 7 business days for Starter/Growth packages) depend on timely receipt of client text, photographs, logo assets, and staging approvals.

2. Payment Terms & Refund / Cancellation Policy

Projects require a 50% booking advance to initiate architecture and design, with the remaining 50% payable upon staging preview approval prior to production domain deployment. If a client cancels within 24 hours of booking before design work begins, the advance is refundable minus payment gateway/processing charges. Once custom design and code engineering milestones are delivered on staging, booking advances are non-refundable.

3. Data Privacy & Security Policy

Contact details submitted via consultation forms (Business Name, Phone Number, City, Industry) are used solely to provide website consultations and project proposals. We never sell or rent client contact data to third-party marketers.

4. Performance & SEO Disclaimer

We engineer websites with clean semantic HTML, fast static/CDN assets, structured schema markup, and on-page local SEO best practices. Search engine rankings and lead volume depend on market competition, domain authority, location, and ongoing client business activity.

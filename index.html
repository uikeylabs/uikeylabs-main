VERIFIABLE ENGINEERING BENCHMARKS

How Our Architecture Solves Common Local Business Website Problems

Below are documented architectural improvements delivered across our Healthcare, Education, and Hospitality build templates (measured via local Lighthouse & payload inspection, September 2026).

HEALTHCARE CLINIC FUNNEL

OPD Appointment Booking Flow

Challenge: Patients struggled with multi-step login forms on mobile phones when trying to book urgent OPD slots.

Engineering Solution: Replaced complex login forms with a 1-screen department & time-slot selector that formats a structured WhatsApp booking message in under 15 seconds.

EDUCATION & SCHOOL PORTAL

Image & Script Payload Optimization

Challenge: Uncompressed campus photos (`>1.0 MB` each) and runtime CSS scripts slowed mobile page loads on 4G networks.

Engineering Solution: Converted hero and gallery assets to WebP (`79 KB` vs `1,018 KB` — 92% reduction) and compiled static CSS (`assets/styles.min.css`).

RESTAURANT & RETAIL CATALOG

Zero-Commission Direct Ordering

Challenge: Local cafes and kirana stores wanted a digital menu and direct UPI QR without paying 25–30% third-party aggregator commissions.

Engineering Solution: Built an interactive item cart that sends itemized orders directly to the owner's WhatsApp along with a direct bank-to-bank UPI QR standee.

TRANSPARENT ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about deliverables, timelines, domain & hosting, revisions, SEO scope, and payment terms.

1. What is included in every website package? +
Every website package includes custom mobile-responsive design, click-to-WhatsApp inquiry integration, Google Maps embed, SSL security certificate configuration, on-page SEO metadata (Title, Meta Description, Open Graph, JSON-LD schema), WebP image optimization, and 30 days of post-launch technical support.
2. What is the realistic launch timeline? +
Starter and Growth Pro websites typically launch within 3 to 7 business days. Exact launch timeline depends on how quickly content (business details, service pricing, photos, logo) and staging approvals are provided by your team.
3. How do domain name and web hosting work? +
You retain 100% ownership of your custom domain name (e.g., .com or .in). We can connect your existing domain or guide you in registering a new one in your own name, and deploy your website on high-speed global cloud hosting (GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or your preferred host).
4. How many design and content revisions are included? +
We include up to 2 structured rounds of revisions during the staging review phase so you can refine text, colors, photos, and package pricing before going live.
5. How does the WhatsApp lead automation work? +
When a visitor fills out your inquiry form or clicks a service/product button, our script validates their inputs and opens WhatsApp with a pre-formatted message addressed directly to your official business WhatsApp number—with zero monthly per-message software fees.
6. What is the SEO scope included? +
We implement complete technical and on-page local SEO foundations: clean heading hierarchy (H1–H3), canonical URLs, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, Open Graph tags, fast mobile loading, and `LocalBusiness` / `ProfessionalService` JSON-LD schema. Search rankings depend on local competition, domain age, and ongoing business activity.
7. What are the payment terms? +
We follow a milestone-based structure: 50% booking advance to initiate project setup and design, and the remaining 50% upon staging link approval prior to final live domain deployment.
8. What is the cancellation and refund policy? +
If you cancel a booking within 24 hours before custom design and coding begin, your advance is refundable (minus any payment gateway fees). Once your custom staging website has been engineered and delivered for review, booking advances cover the completed engineering labor. Full details are available on our Legal Policies page.
9. What support is provided after launch? +
Every website includes 30 days of free post-launch support (60 days on Growth Pro) covering minor text/phone number updates, bug fixes, and SSL/domain health checks. Optional annual maintenance plans are also available.
